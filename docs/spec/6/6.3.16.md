6.3.16
Expiration time for cached @contexts
Implementations shall comply with the Expires header field (section 5.3 of IETF RFC 7234 [30]) or a max-age or
s-maxage response directive of Cache-Control header field (section 5.2.2 of IETF RFC 7234 [30]) that may be present
in the downloaded @context. This means that implementations shall periodically invalidate the "Cached" @contexts
according to the headers mentioned above. Since origin servers do not always provide explicit expiration times,
implementations should assign a heuristic (for instance according to IETF RFC 7234 [30] section 4.2.2) expiration time
when an explicit time is not specified.

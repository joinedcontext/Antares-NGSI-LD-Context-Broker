4.5.20.1
Introduction
NGSI-LD defines a specialized type of Property named VocabProperty, defined by the NGSI-LD @context
described by the present document in clause 4.4.


When dealing with NGSI-LD Entities, implementations shall interpret the JSON-LD nodes of type VocabProperty
as per clause 4.5.20.2 (when in normalized representation) or clause 4.5.20.3 (when in concise representation).
Both normalized and concise representation of VocabProperties shall be supported by implementations and can be
selected by Context Consumers through specific request parameters. An example of this representation can be
found in annex C, clause C.2.2.
